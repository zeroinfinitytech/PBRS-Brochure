01The School

Cookery and hospitality, studied in central Auckland.

PBRS is an NZQA-registered school for cookery, food and beverage service, and hospitality management, established in 2000. Our campus has large buildings and wide, open training spaces in the Auckland CBD.

2.4

New Zealand Diploma in Hospitality Management

Level5
QualificationDiploma
CreditsOn request
Duration1 year

A one-year route into supervisory and business management, through to starting your own business.

EntryAge 18 or older, with Year 12 or NCEA Level 3 (or equivalent), a Level 4 hospitality qualification, or two years of relevant work experience. English test or equivalent accepted.

Student support

Academic guidance, a dedicated Pastoral Care Team, learning support, careers advice and help finding study-related work.

Facilities

Modern classrooms and computer laboratories, professional commercial training kitchens, a training restaurant, a library and a student lounge.
